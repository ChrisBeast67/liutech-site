Air Quality

ISO 8573-1 Air Quality Standards

Our systems meet international air quality classes for various industrial applications.

Class 0

Highest purity for aerospace, pharmaceutical, and food applications. Ultra-low contamination levels.

Max particle: 0.1μm | Moisture: -40°C dew point | Oil: 0.003 mg/m³

Class 1-2

High-purity air for medical, electronics, and precision manufacturing.

Max particle: 1μm | Moisture: -20°C dew point | Oil: 0.01 mg/m³

Class 3-4

Standard industrial air for tools, painting, and general manufacturing.

Max particle: 5μm | Moisture: +3°C dew point | Oil: 0.1 mg/m³

Class 5-6

Basic compressed air for non-critical industrial applications.

Max particle: 40μm | Moisture: +7°C dew point | Oil: 1 mg/m³
